Our Story

From a family kitchen to yours

Chai Adda began with one stubborn belief: that a proper cup of chai — and the conversation around it — is worth doing right.

How it started

Chai was never just a drink

Our founder grew up in Hyderabad, where the day was measured in cups of chai — the first at dawn with his grandmother, the last long after midnight with friends who never quite wanted to leave. The tea was the excuse. The adda — the gathering — was the point.

When he moved to Glasgow, he found brilliant coffee everywhere and almost nowhere to sit with a real masala chai. So he started making it himself: grinding the spices, ruining a few batches, calling home for the ratios.

Chai Adda is what grew from that stove. A place that tastes like home and welcomes everyone into it.

What we stand for

Mission, vision & a tea philosophy

Our Mission

To serve authentic Indian chai and street food with honesty and warmth — making the ritual of a great cup an everyday pleasure, not a special occasion.

Our Vision

An adda in every neighbourhood — welcoming corners where people from every background slow down, connect, and feel they belong over a shared pot of tea.

Tea Philosophy

Whole spices, never powders. A slow simmer, never a shortcut. Single-estate leaves that can stand up to milk and masala. If it wouldn't pass at home, it doesn't leave our kitchen.

The journey

A few cups along the way

2019

A kitchen in Hyderabad

The first masala blend is written down properly — twelve spices, one non-negotiable simmer.

2022

Hello, Glasgow

A move north, a serious craving, and no proper chai in sight. The idea takes hold.

2023

The first pop-up

A weekend market stall sells out of samosas by 11am. We take the hint.

2024

Doors open on Sauchiehall Street

Our first permanent adda — warm wood, soft light, and a stove that never really turns off.

2026

Three cafés and counting

West End and Southside join the family, with the same twelve spices and the same welcome.
