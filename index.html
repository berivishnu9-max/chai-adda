Why Chai Adda

Small details, honest chai

We obsess over the things that matter — the leaves, the milk, the boil, the welcome. Here is what goes into every cup.

Fresh Ingredients

Whole spices ground in-house, full-cream milk and ginger cut fresh each morning. Nothing from a packet.

Premium Tea Leaves

Single-estate Assam and Nilgiri leaves, selected for a bold, malty body that stands up to spice and milk.

Authentic Recipes

Family recipes carried from Hyderabad to Glasgow — the same masala, the same slow simmer, every time.

Freshly Prepared Snacks

Samosas folded and fried through the day, so the pastry is always shattering-crisp and the filling warm.

Comfortable Seating

Warm wood, soft light and room to linger. An adda is a place to stay a while — we built ours that way.

Fast, Friendly Service

Our Story

An adda is more than a café

In India, an adda is a corner where friends gather, plans are made and hours disappear over endless rounds of chai. We missed it. So we built one on Sauchiehall Street.

Every masala blend is ground in our kitchen, every snack made the way our families taught us. No shortcuts, no syrups — just honest chai and a seat that's always yours.

Come for the tea. Stay for the conversation. That's the whole idea.
